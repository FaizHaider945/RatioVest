About RatioVest

The idea

RatioVest started in a Business Finance class. We learned how to read a company’s numbers: can it pay its bills, how much debt does it carry, is it really profitable. Then I noticed that most people who invest never learn this, so they buy on tips and hype.

What it does

You type in a company’s financial figures. RatioVest works out 26 ratios, gives one health score out of 100, checks whether the price looks fair, and lets you compare up to four companies side by side. It also shows common-size and index analysis.

Who built it

M. Faiz Haider, BS FinTech student at Air University Islamabad (H-11 campus). RatioVest was built for the Zindigi Prize 2026.

Where it is going

Live PSX data through a licensed partner, multi-year trend analysis, sector benchmarks, and Urdu explanations for first-time investors.

Legal and privacy

Not financial advice

RatioVest is an educational tool. Its scores and labels (such as “Strong candidate” or “Avoid for now”) are based only on the numbers you type in and general rules of thumb. They are not a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Please do your own research or speak to a licensed adviser.

Limits of the tool

The benchmarks are not tuned to any one sector, so compare companies in the same sector. It is not suitable for banks, insurers or other financial companies. Results are only as accurate as the figures entered.

Privacy

RatioVest has no accounts. The numbers you type, and any PDF you open, are processed in your browser and are not uploaded. If you choose “Save for everyone”, only the extracted numbers, the company name, sector and year are stored in a shared public database, and nothing about you. Your colour theme is saved on your own device. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts and the 3D library from cdnjs, so those services may see that you visited.
